THE BIG PICTURE

A simplified attack lifecycle

Real incidents vary. This model helps organize thinking—not predict every attack.

  1. 01⌕

    Opportunity

    A weakness or exposed resource exists.

    DEFENSE

    Patch systems; reduce exposure.

  2. 02✉

    Initial access

    A person or system is targeted.

    DEFENSE

    Train people; use MFA.

  3. 03⌁

    Impact attempt

    An attacker tries to misuse access.

    DEFENSE

    Limit permissions; monitor activity.

  4. 04◉

    Detection & recovery

    Respond, restore, and learn.

    DEFENSE

    Keep backups; practice response.
